Privacy policy

Keel · last updated 6 November 2026

Keel is a personal app with one user, its developer.

Keel reads its user's Google Calendar and adds its own events to it, only to plan that user's day. The data is stored only on the user's own device. It isn't sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models, and it is passed to other services only as needed for Keel's own features.

Keel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account's third-party connections page.
